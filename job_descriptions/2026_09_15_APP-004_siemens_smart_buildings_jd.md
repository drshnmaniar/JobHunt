## About the Role

Siemens Smart Infrastructure is looking for a committed software developer to join their development team at the intersection of modern software development and innovative building automation. You will develop solutions end-to-end from initial idea, through C# implementation, to the development of expansion modules for complex systems such as Desigo CC (Siemens flagship Building Management System).

---

## Responsibilities

- Development and maintenance of software solutions based on modern technologies with a primary focus on C#
- Holistic cooperation in the further development of existing systems and architectures in the field of smart buildings
- Ensure high software quality by implementing unit testing and consistent application of Clean Code and Secure-by-Design Principles
- Design and implementation of interfaces in an interdisciplinary and international environment
- Support in the configuration and optimization of CI/CD pipelines and modern DevOps practices
- Participation in the integration of building automation systems (e.g. via TIA Portal or Desigo CC)
